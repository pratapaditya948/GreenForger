Frequently asked questions

Can any plant be grown hydroponically?

Many can. Leafy greens, herbs, tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers and strawberries do especially well. Large root crops and trees are harder to grow this way.

Does hydroponics really use less water?

Usually, yes. The water and nutrients are recycled through the system instead of draining into the ground, so far less is wasted.

How much space do I need to start?

Very little. A balcony or a small corner of a terrace is enough for a starter setup, and you can expand later.

Is hydroponic food safe and nutritious?

Yes. Plants take up the same nutrients they would from soil, and because there is no soil, fewer soil pests and diseases reach the crop.

Is hydroponic produce the same as organic?

Not automatically. Organic is a certification with its own rules. Hydroponic produce can be grown with very little or no pesticide, but check what a grower actually uses.

How long does it take for plants to grow?

It depends on the crop. Lettuce and herbs can be ready in a few weeks, while fruiting plants like tomatoes take a few months.

Does a hydroponic system need electricity?

Most systems use a small pump to move water, and indoor setups use lights. Simple passive systems can run with little or no power.

How much does it cost to set up?

A small home kit costs far less than a commercial farm. Costs depend on size, equipment and crops, so contact us for a quote.
